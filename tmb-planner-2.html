about resupply towns Courmayeur, Les Chapieux, La Fouly and Champex-Lac all have shops where you can buy food along the way, so you don't need to carry the whole trip's supplies from the start. about alternate routes Several sections of the TMB have a well-known high-route or pass variant alongside the standard route (Col de Tricot, Col des Fours, the Mont de la Saxe high route, Col Petit Ferret, Fenêtre d'Arpette, the Charamillon side via Le Tour, and Grand Balcon Sud via Lac Blanc). By default this page measures the standard route. Use the "via" dropdown on a given day to force it to route over one of these alternates instead — move your cursor over or tap the distance for a day to see the full list of places along that day's route. If you stay at a stop that only exists on a variant (e.g. Refuge de Miage or Lac Blanc), that day's via option is locked to the matching variant, and stops on the standard side (e.g. Les Chapieux) lock it to the standard route. about the Auto buttons Clicking Auto CW or Auto CCW generates a suggested itinerary around the standard TMB loop (clockwise or counter-clockwise) using your current trip duration and chosen start/finish towns, spacing overnight stops as evenly as possible. It always plans the standard route — pick alternates afterward using each day's via dropdown if you want them.

Distances and elevation figures here were manually compiled and may contain small errors — double-check anything trip-critical against a current guidebook or map before you go. This tool does not yet track refuge/gîte booking availability; TMB accommodation should be reserved individually and well in advance.
